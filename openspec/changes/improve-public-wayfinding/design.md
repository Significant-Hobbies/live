## Direction

Preserve the Life Atlas: warm paper, a large yellow field, one coral directional
marker, editorial serif type, and dark ink. The page should feel like reaching
the edge of a map, not encountering a server error. One primary route returns
home; two secondary routes continue exploration.

Hobby pages remain data-driven through `HOBBY_CATEGORIES`. A focused invariant
test proves every catalog name maps to a unique slug and back to a category,
rather than duplicating 122 page files.
