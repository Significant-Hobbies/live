## Design Specificity Verdict

The Life Atlas is now structural rather than lexical: desktop uses an asymmetric branching route, mobile uses a vertical trail, and only three territories appear before deliberate expansion. The page ends with the user's own dreams and a first-door action instead of an appended dashboard or game menu.

## What's Working

- Confidence-only native results protect trust; unsupported dreams remain exact and open wider-world research.
- The focused route connects discovery, preservation, and one smaller doorway without gamification or shame.
- Contextual accessible names, 44px targets, responsive reflow, and zero axe violations support inclusive operation.

## Resolved Priority Issues

- **Resolved P1:** The operating-model overload was removed.
- **Resolved P1:** Weak related results were replaced by confidence-only native results and honest external paths.
- **Resolved P1:** The equal-card catalogue became a branching, progressively revealed atlas.

## Remaining Observations

- **P2:** Saved items still lack an immediate undo.
- **P2:** A returning mobile user may prefer the personal atlas before discovery.
- **P3:** Numbered territories can imply sequence even though they are parallel clusters.
- **P3:** “419 native possibilities” remains slightly inventory-led.

## Questions to Consider

- Should a non-empty personal atlas move above discovery for returning users?
- Would a short undo window improve save confidence without adding management weight?
